TowerZ Feature : Living strategy and plan
-----------------------------------------
The strategy stays alive as the business changes.

TowerZ keeps your plan connected to revenue, client signals, delivery reality, and new opportunities so the next move stays grounded in what is happening now.

Instead of freezing the strategy in a document, TowerZ keeps priorities, roadmap, and follow-up ready to adjust when the business shifts.

- Signals come back into the plan: Diagnostics, finances, client activity, and market context feed the next review instead of living in separate tools.
- Adjust the next move, not from scratch: Re-rank priorities, shift milestones, and rebalance focus without rebuilding the whole plan every time.
- You keep the final say: TowerZ prepares the adjustment, keeps the rationale visible, and leaves approval with you before anything changes downstream.

TowerZ Feature : Decision engine
--------------------------------
TowerZ keeps the records the work depends on

Client files, invoices, payments, taxes, documents, and activity history are created inside TowerZ, not reconstructed in a fresh chat every time.
- Client records and relationship history
- Invoices, payments, and tax context
- Documents and operating references
- Tasks, follow-ups, and activity history

Recommendations are prepared, not silently executed
TowerZ can surface a next move, draft the work, and queue it for review. You decide what gets approved.

1. Signal: A recommendation is prepared from the records already in TowerZ.
2. Review: The draft action stays ready for review instead of going out on its own.
3. Approval: You approve what should move forward and keep control over sensitive actions.

-----------------------------------
Agents work from your real business context, not from a blank prompt.

These agents work from records already inside TowerZ and keep approvals with you. They prepare concrete next steps, not generic advice.
